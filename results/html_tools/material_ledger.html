Reading the ledger. Burden is the cradle-to-gate impact of producing 1 kg of a material. Benefit only exists for the climate change category, and only for biobased materials: product-allocated materials earn a sequestration credit (GWPbio, Guest et al. 2012), while waste and wild-harvested materials earn a delayed-emissions credit (dynamic characterization factor, Levasseur et al. 2010). A negative balance means the recipe is a net climate sink over the chosen lifetime; a positive balance means it's a net source.

Baselines. Concrete and Steel are shown in the unit chart for reference regardless of what's in your recipe — they carry no benefit mechanism.
