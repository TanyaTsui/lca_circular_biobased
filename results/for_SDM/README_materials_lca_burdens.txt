This file explains the LCA results of a list of materials

"material_name": name of material
"amount": amount of material that the LCA was done on (this is part of the functional unit)
"unit": unit of the amount of material that the LCA was done on (this is part of the functional unit)
"impact_category": environmental impact category. this specifies the type of environmental impact we are assessing 
"impact_category_unit": units for the environmental impact cateogry
"impact_score": score for the environmental impact 
"lca_method": method used for the LCA. There are multiple methods for assessing environmental impact, so it's important to specify which method was used, to make sure that results are comparable.
"lca_database": database that was used for the LCA. Most often this is the ecoinvent database. There are multiple databases for doing LCA, so it's important to specify, to ensure comparability.
